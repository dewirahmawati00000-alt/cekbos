About Custom Pewter

Custom Pewter specializes in crafting premium custom medals using high-quality materials such as Zinc Alloy and durable metal alloy blends. Our medals are designed to look elegant, feel solid in hand, and deliver the level of quality suitable for competitions, corporate events, and international-standard awards.

With a production process that combines precision molding, fine detailing, and expert finishing, every medal we create showcases outstanding clarity, depth, and durability. Whether you need detailed 2D/3D medals, custom shapes, or branded premium medals — we turn your ideas into high-quality metal creations.

  • • Premium Zinc Alloy (High Detail + Strong Durability)
  • • Standard Metal Alloy (Economical + Good Quality)
  • • High-precision Custom Molding (2D & 3D)
  • • Custom Plating Colors: Gold, Silver, Bronze, Black Nickel
